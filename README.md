## About

I test web, mobile and API products for startups and growing teams across e-commerce, healthcare, fintech-style B2B platforms, AI products, education and live video.

With **31+ documented projects**, my work covers the full QA cycle: requirement analysis, test design, functional and regression testing, defect reporting, retesting and release recommendations. I have worked as **QA Lead** on several projects, coordinating testing and reviewing bug reports written by QA interns and students.

Before QA, I spent **12 years in banking** — from cashier-operator and precious metals appraiser to customer service and **credit risk officer**. That background shaped how I test today: attention to detail, careful verification of data and a risk-based view of what can go wrong.

## QA Focus

- **Web and mobile testing:** End-to-end user flows on web, iOS and Android, including cross-device scenarios (mobile ↔ web), real devices and emulators.
- **Data and business logic:** Financial calculations, invoices and reports, statuses, validation rules and consistency across interconnected modules.
- **Access and security-related flows:** Authentication and social login, OTP limits, role-based permissions (Owner, Admin, Member, Guest) and workspace data isolation.
- **AI-powered features:** AI chat and search grounding, AI-assisted workflows, language consistency and error handling when AI services fail.

## Documentation & Quality

My work extends beyond finding bugs: every project in the portfolio includes **bug reports, test cases, test scenarios, checklists and a requirements traceability matrix (RTM)**. For projects I led, I also prepared **test plans and test summary reports** with risks and release recommendations.

I follow a defined [defect life cycle](https://github.com/LiliImerlishvili/QA-Portfolio/blob/master/QA-Processes/Defect-Life-Cycle.md), retest fixes using developers' root-cause notes and keep regression checklists up to date after every release.

**Currently building:** UI automation with Playwright and preparing for the ISTQB CTFL 4.0 certification.

## Reviewing Bug Reports

I review bug reports written by **QA interns and students** before they reach developers — checking reproducibility, structure, environment details and the difference between a bug and a recommendation, and giving specific feedback on the sections that need improvement. My review process is documented [here](https://github.com/LiliImerlishvili/QA-Portfolio/blob/master/QA-Processes/Bug-Report-Review-Process.md).
